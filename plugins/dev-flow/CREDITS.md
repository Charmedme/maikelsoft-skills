# Credits

The method of `plan-and-build` comes from six skills of Matt Pocock: grill-me (with grilling), to-spec, to-tickets, to-questionnaire, prototype, and implement. The source is [github.com/mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills).

The text of `plan-and-build` is new. It joins the six skills into one flow and writes the rules in ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English. These ideas come from the source skills:

- The design tree, the frontier, and a recommended answer for each question (grilling).
- The spec template with user stories, decisions, test seams, and out of scope (to-spec).
- Tracer-bullet tickets with blocking edges, and expand-contract for wide changes (to-tickets).
- The questionnaire for a third person (to-questionnaire).
- The logic prototype as one HTML file, and the UI prototype with variants and a switch bar (prototype).
- Test-driven development at the agreed seams, and a review against the spec (implement).
