# Laws and standards

Status on 2026-10-25. Check the current status before you write it in a report. This is not legal advice.

| Item | Status |
|------|--------|
| European Accessibility Act (EAA), Directive (EU) 2019/882 | Applies since 2025-06-28 to the products and services in its scope, for example e-commerce, banking, transport tickets, and e-books. Each member state enforces it with its own law. Micro-enterprises that give services are exempt. |
| Web Accessibility Directive (EU) 2016/2102 | Applies to the websites and apps of public bodies. |
| EN 301 549 V3.2.1 | The version cited in the Official Journal. It uses WCAG 2.1 A and AA. Conformity with it gives a presumption of conformity. |
| EN 301 549 V4.1.1 | Adopted on 2026-08-24, published on 2026-09-02. It uses WCAG 2.2 A and AA (6 new criteria) and maps its clauses to the EAA. Deque expects the citation in the Official Journal around 2026-11-30. Only after the citation does it give a presumption of conformity. |
| WCAG 2.2 | W3C Recommendation. It has 6 new A and AA criteria. These are 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (Minimum), 2.5.7 Dragging Movements, 2.5.8 Target Size (Minimum), 3.2.6 Consistent Help, 3.3.7 Redundant Entry, and 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication (Minimum). 4.1.1 Parsing is removed. |

## What to write in a report

- The target: WCAG 2.2 AA. A design that meets it also meets WCAG 2.1 AA, because WCAG 2.2 adds criteria and removes only 4.1.1.
- The checked version and the date of the check.
- "The checked states pass the checked criteria", not "the product conforms".
