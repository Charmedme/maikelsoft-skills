# Product: Naar de Kloten

## What it is

A Dutch party card game for phones. One player is the host: the host starts a session and shares a 6-letter code or a QR code. The other players join with the code. Each round, one player is the leider (leader). The leider reads a stelling (a black card with a blank) aloud. The other players each choose one white card from a hand of 7 cards. The leider reads the cards aloud and chooses the funniest card. That player gets a point. The first player with the target score wins (default 7 points).

Special card: the Botte Schande. A player can play it once per game to swap the own hand.

## Users

- Groups of friends from 18 to 35 years, at a party or on a video call. They play in the evening, often with music and drinks.
- They hold the phone in one hand. They look up at the other players often. A round takes 1 to 2 minutes.
- The host sets up the game while the others wait. The host often plays music through the game.
- On a video call, players use the chat and the microphone of the game.

## Facts

- 3 to 10 players. Most games have 4 to 6 players (app statistics, 2026 Q3).
- 92% of the sessions are on phones (390 to 430 px wide). 6% on tablets (820 px). 2% on a laptop.
- 31% of the sessions are remote (video call).
- Dark rooms: most games start after 21:00.
- The game must meet WCAG 2.2 AA (owner rule).

## Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Wachtkamer | The lobby: players join and the host sets up the game |
| Sessiecode | The 6-letter code to join |
| Stelling | The black card with a blank, read by the leider |
| Kaart | A white answer card in the hand |
| Leider | The player who reads and chooses this round |
| Stand | The scoreboard |
| Botte Schande | The special card that swaps the own hand |

## Voice

Cheeky and fast, never mean to a player. Short words. Dutch only.
