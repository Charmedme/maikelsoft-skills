## T6. Accessibility review

A copy of the built FL-31 app with 6 accessibility faults that axe-core does not find (`fixtures/fieldline-app-a11y/DEFECTS.md`). The short prompt: "review the built feature against the approved design". A Sonnet reader maps the findings to the faults.

| Fault | Baseline | Previous (0.2.0) | New v1 | New v2 |
|-------|----------|------------------|--------|--------|
| A1 No visible focus on the chips | 3 of 3 | 2 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 |
| A2 Focus trap in the side panel | 1 of 3 | 2 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 |
| A3 Focus under the fixed selection bar | 2 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 |
| A4 Animation runs with reduced motion | 1 of 3 | 1 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 |
| A5 `tabindex="1"` on overdue links | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 1 of 3 | 3 of 3 |
| A6 Toast without a live region | 2 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 | 3 of 3 |
| Total | 12 of 18 | 14 of 18 | 16 of 18 | 18 of 18 |
| Cost for each run | $0.43 | $1.01 | $0.87 | $0.98 |

On the clean app, v2 has 2 P0 or P1 findings in each run. The previous version has 2 and 4. These are the 2 real faults of the clean app that batch 2 found. False findings: 0 to 1 in each run for both.

Findings:

1. The tool did most of the work. The new Tab walk of `uxcheck.py review` finds A1 to A5 on the fixture, and nothing on the clean app. The manual checklist of the skill finds A6 (no tool finds a missing live region).
2. v1 missed A5 two times. The tool gave only a warning (the focus jumps back), and the agent blamed the toast. v2 adds the rule `RV-TABINDEX` (a tabindex above 0 is an error), and then all 3 runs find A5.
3. The baseline is cheap, but it gives no verdict and no severity, as in batch 2.
